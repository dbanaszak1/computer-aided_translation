For goods not subject to registration, it is recommended to state in English that it is not required, e.g., "UIT not required" or "UITFREE."

Ladies and Gentlemen,
We would like to inform you of the planned changes to DHL eCommerce (Poland) Sp. z o.o.
In order to provide customers with excellent service, the DHL Group has divisions focused on providing specialized service.
In Poland, within DHL eCommerce (Poland) Sp. z o.o., there are separate divisions: DHL  eCommerce and DHL Freight.
The divisions function independently of each other as organized parts of the company, capable of providing the highest quality services to customers independently.
In order to make it easier for our Customers and Contractors to work with individual Divisions and to streamline the work within each Division, we have decided to formally divide the Company, thanks to which each Division will obtain its own legal personality.
Below is the basic range of services provided by each division.
The Parcel Division (eCommerce) primarily provides transportation and courier services for shipments weighing up to 31.5 kg. and transportation services for heavier general cargo shipments weighing up to 2,500 kg. including pallets.
It operates on the territory of Poland (domestic shipments) and on the territory of European Union countries (international road transport and courier services).
The Fracht Division mainly performs domestic and international freight forwarding and road transportation of shipments, including full-vehicle (i.e., carrying only goods belonging to one customer on a given vehicle) and partial-vehicle transportation services.
Once the Company is formally demerged in accordance with the attached demerger plan, the Fracht Division will be transferred to the demerged company.
The newly formed company will operate under the nameDHL Freight (Poland) Sp. z o.o.with its registered office in Warsaw.
On the other hand, Dywizja Parcel (eCommerce) will remain in DHL eCommerce Polska Sp. z o.o., which, after division, will be renamed DHL eCommerce (Poland) Sp. z o.o.
We assure you that the process of splitting the Company will not be felt by our Customers and Contractors.
The way we provide services and the business terms of our cooperation will remain unchanged.
Best regards,
Managing Director of DHL eCommerce - Agnieszka Swierszcz
Managing Director of DHL Fracht - Marek Skowronski
